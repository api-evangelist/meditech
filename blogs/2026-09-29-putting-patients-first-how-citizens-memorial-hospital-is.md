This is a period of rapid change in the healthcare landscape, and at Citizens Memorial Hospital we are going through an exciting time of intense transformation and rapid growth in response. In 2026 alone, we opened a brand-new hospital, launched a new dialysis center, and acquired an oncology center. All of that is in addition to our 34 primary and specialty clinics, seven long-term care facilities, and multiple specialized trauma and stroke centers.
