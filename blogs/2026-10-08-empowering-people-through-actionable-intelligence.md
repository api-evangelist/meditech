Healthcare organizations face more challenges than ever, and finding the right path forward requires collaboration, innovation, and a clear vision. This was the driving force behind MEDITECH LIVE 26 , held from September 29 to October 1 in Foxborough, MA. Over three days of discussion and problem solving from healthcare leaders , attendees from around the world explored how to successfully leverage data into meaningful, actionable intelligence — while empowering the human element in an increasingly AI-driven healthcare landscape.
