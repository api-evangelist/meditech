The seemingly simple act of listening can lead to great innovation. Outpatient therapy practices operate under intense pressure, so by listening to the frontline realities of physical, occupational, and speech therapists, we developed Expanse Outpatient Therapy, an extension of our Ambulatory solution , to directly address the systemic operational hurdles holding therapy clinics back.
